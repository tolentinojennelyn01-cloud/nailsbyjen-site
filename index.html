FAQ & Policies

Do I need to pay a deposit? +

Yes, a ₱50 deposit is needed to confirm your slot, payable via GCash. You can scan the QR code below any time. STRICTLY NO REFUND

Can I reschedule or cancel? +

Yes — just message Jen on Facebook as soon as you know so she can adjust the schedule for other clients.

What's the difference between Home Service and Home Base? +

Home Base means you come to Jen's place in Paliparan 2, Dasmariñas. Home Service means Jen travels to you, with a small travel fee based on distance, confirmed once she reviews your address.

How do I confirm my booking? +

Fill out the booking form, download your receipt, and send it to Jen on Facebook Messenger. She'll confirm your date, time, and deposit details there.
